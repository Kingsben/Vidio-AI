# Animation film credits

These are independent animation works embedded from Wikimedia Commons. They are not Vidio AI creations, generated outputs, customer work, or demonstrations of Vidio AI generation. Video and poster files are streamed from Wikimedia Commons; the project does not download or edit the films.

The player streams Wikimedia Commons' 480p VP9 WebM transcodes (VP9 video with Opus audio) of each source file. These are Wikimedia-generated delivery transcodes; Vidio AI does not cut, re-encode, crop, or otherwise modify the films. The Big Buck Bunny Commons file is itself a 29.6-second excerpt; the other listed files are the Commons-published films. Only the selected film is attached to the video player; other films are represented by lazily loaded thumbnails. The player uses `preload="none"` on mobile/reduced-motion contexts and requests metadata on desktop, where one muted, controllable preview may autoplay. Selecting a film replaces the single player source rather than loading all five videos. The player poster is 1280px wide; gallery thumbnails are approximately 330px wide and loaded lazily from Commons.
